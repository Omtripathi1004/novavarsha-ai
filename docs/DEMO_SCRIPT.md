# NovaVarsha AI — 3-Minute Judge & Demo Flow Script
**Lenovo Hackathon 2026**

This guided demo script follows the end-to-end product narrative:
**Observe → Understand → Predict → Explain → Recommend → Simulate → Act**

---

### Phase 1: Orientation & Sovereignty (0:00 – 0:30)
1. **Header & Sovereign Identity**: Point out official Survey of India boundary adherence. Show that Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, and Arunachal Pradesh are rendered in full sovereign integrity with zero third-party border compromises.
2. **Role Selection**: Highlight the Role Switcher (**Farmer/User**, **Agriculture Officer**, **Administrator/Developer**).
3. **Data Mode**: Show the Live Mode / Cached Mode / Demo Mode toggle (clearly labelled; never faking live data).

### Phase 2: Hyperlocal Geospatial Intelligence (0:30 – 1:00)
1. **6 Map Modes**: Switch between `Mappls Street View`, `Mappls Hydro-GIS` (river basin cartography), `Mappls Terrain`, `Mappls Portal`, `Satellite View`, and `Hybrid View`.
2. **8 Agro-Climatic Hubs**: Click on *Vidarbha Cotton Belt (Nagpur)* or *Gangetic Basin (Lucknow)*. The map dynamically flies to the coordinates, and the Telemetry HUD updates with live metrics (rainfall anomaly, temperature, elevation).
3. **Layer Toggles & Time Slider**: Toggle Rainfall Anomaly, Crop Suitability, and Water Stress layers. Drag the time slider to inspect forecast progression.

### Phase 3: Explainable Crop Intelligence & XAI (1:00 – 1:45)
1. **ICAR Cultivar Ranking**: Switch to the **Agriculture / Smart Crop** tab. Inspect the top recommended cultivars (e.g. *Swarna MTU-7029* or *Sahbhagi Dhan*).
2. **Multi-Factor Score**: View the transparent breakdown across Thermal Suitability, Rain Timing, Water Need, and Soil Moisture.
3. **"Why Not?" Excluded Crops**: Expand the diagnostic drawer to see exactly why certain crops (e.g. *Cotton*, *Groundnut*) were rejected for this zone/season.
4. **SHAP Waterfall**: Open the **Explainable AI (XAI)** tab to inspect the Shapley attribution waterfall showing how ENSO ONI, IOD, and local rainfall impacted the risk score.

### Phase 4: What-If Agriculture Scenario Lab (1:45 – 2:15)
1. **Simulation**: Open the **Scenario Lab**. Adjust the **Rainfall Deficit** slider to `-30%` and **Temperature Offset** to `+2.5°C`.
2. **Instant Recalculation**: Watch the suitability score drop and composite risk score rise in real time.
3. **Plain-Language Agronomic Impact**: Point out the automated diagnosis: *"A 30% rainfall deficit during vegetative phase triggers 14-day dry break risk; shift to short-duration drought hardy cultivar (Sahbhagi Dhan)."*
4. **Sensitivity Ranking**: Review which parameter caused the most severe stress impact.

### Phase 5: AI Assistant (RAG) & Decision Report (2:15 – 3:00)
1. **Context-Aware RAG**: Click the Floating AI Assistant widget. Click the quick prompt: *"What should I do if a 10-day dry break hits during flowering?"*
2. **Evidence Drawer**: Show the source reference citation badge (`DOC-CRIDA-02`) and inspect the authentic ICAR/CRIDA contingency passage.
3. **Voice Audio Synthesis**: Click the audio listen button to hear the synthesized agronomic response in English/Hindi.
4. **1-Click Executive Decision Report**: Click **Generate Executive Report**. Show the print/PDF-ready layout complete with district telemetry, risk breakdown, cultivar recommendations, data freshness timestamps, and official disclaimers.
